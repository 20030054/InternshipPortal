# M09 — Verification and grading

## Purpose

Per-deliverable verification with a mandatory method (BR-11), the
three-item checklist gate (BR-10 — this is the module that finally
replaces `stubGuard("BR-10")` and `stubGuard("BR-11")` on rows 9 and 10
with real guards, now that all three deliverables have somewhere to come
from: M06's documents, M08's evaluation), grade recommendation by the
Focal Person and award by the HoD (rows 11-13), the same-person block
(BR-12, already real since M04 — this module gives it its first real
caller), and grade immutability with the reversal-with-Dean-signature
mechanism (BR-14).

**Depends on:** M01 (`Verification`/`Grade`/`GradeReversal`, all unused
until now), M04 (`recommenderNotAwarder` — real since M04, first real
caller here), M06 (offer letter / completion certificate `Document`
rows), M08 (the `Evaluation` row).

## Scope decisions this module makes

- **The supervisor evaluation's "verification" is its own token
  mechanism, not a `Verification` row.** `Verification.documentId` is a
  foreign key to `Document` — structurally, only `Document`-backed
  deliverables (offer letter, completion certificate) can ever have one.
  BR-11's fixed method list includes `SUPERVISOR_LINK_CONFIRMED`, which
  reads as *a way to verify a document* (e.g. corroborating the
  completion certificate against what the supervisor's evaluation says)
  — not a claim that the evaluation itself needs a second, separate
  verification step. The evaluation's own authenticity is already
  stronger than a manual checkbox: an HMAC-signed, single-use token
  (M08). BR-10's "all three deliverables exist" checks the `Evaluation`
  row's existence directly; BR-11's "all deliverables verified" only
  ever requires `Verification` rows for the two `Document`-backed ones.
- **`recommendedGradeValue`/`recommendedBy` land on `Case`, not a new
  table.** `Grade` (M01) requires `recommendedBy` *and* `awardedBy`
  simultaneously — it can only be created once, atomically, at award
  time; there's nowhere to park "what the Focal Person recommended"
  while the case sits in `GRADE_RECOMMENDED` waiting on the HoD. Two new
  nullable `Case` columns hold that state (mirroring M05's
  `workDescription`/`relevanceConfirmed` and M07's `actualStart`/
  `actualEnd` — the established pattern for "state captured mid-flow,
  consumed by a later step" in this schema) rather than a new
  `GradeRecommendation` table, since nothing else ever needs to query
  "pending recommendations" as their own resource.
- **The HoD's award can differ from the Focal Person's recommendation.**
  "The Focal Person recommends; the HoD awards" (BR-12) reads as two
  independent judgements, not a rubber stamp — the award route accepts
  its own `value`, doesn't silently copy `recommendedGradeValue`. The
  transition target (row 12 `CLOSED_PASS` vs. row 13
  `CLOSED_INCOMPLETE`) follows whatever the HoD actually chooses.
- **A new capability, `grade.reverse` (DEAN) — a real gap in
  `MASTER_PROMPT.md` §3's eighteen rows, not an invention.** BR-14
  requires "a Dean signature" for a grade reversal, but no row in the
  capability table covers it — the same situation M06 hit with
  downloads and M08 hit with evaluation visibility, resolved the same
  way there (reuse where a fit exists) is not available here: none of
  the Dean's other capabilities (`escalation.rule_restart`,
  `waiver.approve_final`) are a defensible stand-in for "sign a grade
  reversal." Added as capability nineteen rather than silently checked
  some other way, so the matrix stays the single source of truth it
  claims to be.
- **A reversal never touches `cases.state`.** No row in M04's transition
  table takes a case out of `CLOSED_PASS`/`CLOSED_INCOMPLETE`, and BR-15
  is explicit: "`CLOSED_PASS` can never be reopened by any role in this
  system." A `GradeReversal` is read as a permanent, additive
  correction to the *record* (the grade row's real-world validity is
  now disputed, visible forever alongside the original) — not a
  re-opening of the case for a new outcome. If a student needs an actual
  second attempt, that is the restart gate's job (M10), a structurally
  separate mechanism already gated on `CLOSED_INCOMPLETE`, not on a
  reversal.
- **`grade_reversals` gets the same append-only privilege treatment as
  `grades`.** M01 revoked `UPDATE`/`DELETE` on `grades` for `scit_app`
  but not on `grade_reversals` — a real, minor gap: a reversal record
  that could itself be edited or deleted would undercut the audit trail
  it exists to provide. Closed in this module's migration.
- **Row 9's auto-chain (`DOCS_PENDING → PENDING_VERIFICATION`, SYSTEM)
  is triggered from the two routes that can complete the checklist**
  — M06's completion-certificate upload and M08's evaluation
  submission — rather than left for a separate sweep. Both already exist
  and already know the case they just touched; a
  `advanceToVerificationIfReady()` call at the end of each (swallowing
  "not ready yet" or "already advanced by a concurrent path," re-raising
  anything else) is a small, targeted addition, consistent with M05's
  same reasoning for rows 3 and 7 — nothing describes a meaningful
  waiting state once the third deliverable actually arrives.
- **Row 10 (`PENDING_VERIFICATION → VERIFIED`) is a distinct, explicit
  Focal Person action, not auto-chained.** Its actor is `FOCAL`, not
  `SYSTEM` — MASTER_PROMPT.md's own transition table (§5.2, mirrored in
  M04's) already draws this line: verifying every deliverable
  individually doesn't itself declare the case fully verified, the Focal
  Person does, once satisfied. `POST /api/cases/:id/mark-verified`
  reuses `deliverable.verify` (no dedicated capability exists for this
  either, and it's the same actor/judgement `deliverable.verify` already
  covers, unlike the `grade.reverse` gap above).
- **OQ-02 stays open; the restrictive default is "any single listed
  method is sufficient, no cross-checking required."** BR-11 requires
  selecting *a* method from the fixed list, not a specific one or a
  minimum count — the master prompt gives the Focal Person's judgement
  no additional constraint, and OQ-02 (still unanswered) is exactly the
  question of whether it should. Implementing a stricter rule now would
  be guessing an answer nobody has given.

## Data touched

One migration on the existing schema:

- `cases.recommended_grade_value "GradeValue" NULL` and
  `cases.recommended_by UUID NULL` (new) — see "Scope decisions."
- `REVOKE UPDATE, DELETE ON "grade_reversals" FROM scit_app` (new,
  closing the gap noted above).

No changes to `Verification`/`Grade`/`GradeReversal`'s own columns, or
to any existing constraint — M01 already built exactly the shape this
module's core actions need.

## Business rules enforced

- **BR-10** (real, replacing M04's stub on row 9) — `deliverablesPresent`
  guard: an `ACTIVE` `OFFER_LETTER` document, an `ACTIVE`
  `COMPLETION_CERTIFICATE` document, and a submitted `Evaluation` (via a
  `used` `SupervisorToken`) all exist for the case.
- **BR-11** (real, replacing M04's stub on row 10) — `deliverablesVerified`
  guard: both `Document`-backed deliverables have at least one
  `Verification` row; the evaluation's existence alone satisfies its own
  leg (see "Scope decisions").
- **BR-12** (already real since M04) — `recommenderNotAwarder` gets its
  first real caller: the award route's context threads
  `recommendedBy`/`awardedBy` from `Case.recommendedBy` and the acting
  HoD's identity.
- **BR-13** — already enforced structurally (`GradeValue` is a native
  Postgres enum, M01); nothing new needed.
- **BR-14** — `POST /api/grades/:id/reverse` (DEAN only, mandatory
  reason) creates an additive `GradeReversal` row; the original `Grade`
  row is never touched, provably so at the database-privilege level
  (`REVOKE UPDATE, DELETE`, M01) as well as by this module never issuing
  an `UPDATE` against it in the first place.
- **BR-15** — no transition exists anywhere that leaves `CLOSED_PASS`;
  a reversal doesn't add one.

## API routes

All mutating routes call `requireCapability()` before touching data.

| Method | Route | Capability | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `POST` | `/api/documents/:id/verify` | `deliverable.verify` (FOCAL) | Body: `method`, `note?`. Document's case must be `PENDING_VERIFICATION`. |
| `GET` | `/api/cases/:id/verifications` | `case.view_own` / `case.view_any` | Every verification recorded for the case's documents. |
| `POST` | `/api/cases/:id/mark-verified` | `deliverable.verify` (FOCAL) | Fires row 10; `409` if BR-11 isn't satisfied yet, `422` with reasons if the guard itself rejects. |
| `POST` | `/api/cases/:id/recommend-grade` | `grade.recommend` (FOCAL) | Body: `value` (`P`/`I`), `reason`. Fires row 11. |
| `POST` | `/api/cases/:id/award-grade` | `grade.award` (HOD) | Body: `value` (`P`/`I`), `reason` (required only for `I`, per row 13). Creates the immutable `Grade` row; fires row 12 or 13 depending on `value`. |
| `POST` | `/api/grades/:id/reverse` | `grade.reverse` (DEAN, new) | Body: `reason`. Additive only — never updates the `Grade` row. |

## Test list

- `unit/deliverable-checklist.test.ts` — `deliverablesPresent()`/
  `deliverablesVerified()` against plain fact objects (which documents/
  evaluation exist, which have verifications): every combination of
  missing/present, no database.
- `integration/BR10_deliverables_present_gate.test.ts` — row 9 fires
  automatically once the third deliverable (completion certificate or
  evaluation, whichever arrives last) is submitted through the real M06/
  M08 routes; does not fire with only two of three present.
- `integration/BR11_deliverables_verified_gate.test.ts` — `mark-verified`
  rejected until both documents are individually verified; succeeds once
  they are, regardless of which of the four methods was used for each.
- `integration/M09_grade_recommend_and_award.test.ts` — the full row
  11-13 arc: recommend requires a reason and a real value; award creates
  the `Grade` row with the right `recommendedBy`/`awardedBy`/`value`;
  award targets `CLOSED_PASS` or `CLOSED_INCOMPLETE` correctly depending
  on the HoD's chosen value; `CLOSED_INCOMPLETE` requires a reason,
  `CLOSED_PASS` doesn't (M04's existing row shapes).
- `integration/BR12_same_person_cannot_recommend_and_award.test.ts` —
  this module's own stated done criterion, named after the rule: a user
  holding both FOCAL and HOD roles who recommends a grade cannot then
  award it on the same case, proven through the real routes end to end
  (not just the guard in isolation, which `guards.test.ts` already
  covers from M04).
- `integration/BR14_grade_reversal.test.ts` — a direct `UPDATE` against
  `grades` fails at the privilege level (mirrors `BR14_grades_immutable.
  test.ts` from M01, confirming M09 didn't accidentally regress it); a
  DEAN can reverse with a reason; a non-Dean cannot; `cases.state` is
  unchanged by a reversal, including for a `CLOSED_PASS` case (BR-15).

## Done criteria

1. A user holding both Focal and HoD roles cannot complete both halves
   on one case — `MASTER_PROMPT.md`'s own stated done criterion,
   proven through the real routes.
2. Rows 9 and 10 have real guards; no stub remains anywhere in the
   transition table except BR-10's own dependency chain being fully
   satisfied (it now is — BR-10/11 were the last two).
3. A grade reversal is additive only, provably so both by the DB
   privilege grant and by the service never issuing an `UPDATE`.
4. `docker compose up --build` succeeds from a clean volume state.
5. `/docs/PROGRESS.md` updated, M09 checked off.
